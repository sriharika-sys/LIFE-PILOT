Chapter 1

Introduction to LifePilot

LifePilot is a local-first personal life-management platform designed to unify tasks, calendar events, daily habits, strategic goals, notes, focus sessions, and analytics in a single browser workspace.

1.1 What Makes LifePilot Different

Multi-Role Adaptability

Select one or more life roles—such as Student, Working Professional, Farmer, Homemaker, Freelancer, or Custom Role—and tailor templates immediately.

100% Local-First Privacy

No sign-up wall, passwords, or remote server required. All records persist inside your browser's localStorage.

Deterministic Intelligence

Smart tools (Energy Planner, Smart Plan B, Local Assistant, Weekly Review) run deterministically on your own records.

1.2 Workspace Navigation Overview

LifePilot provides 13 dedicated views accessible from the left Desktop Sidebar (or the More drawer in Mobile Navigation):

Section Views Included Primary Purpose
Core Workspace Dashboard, Tasks, Calendar, Notes, Habits, Goals Day-to-day execution, scheduling, reference knowledge, streak logging, and long-term milestones.
Productivity & Growth Focus Center, Energy Planner, Weekly Review, Analytics, AI Assistant, Recycle Bin, Settings Deep-work timers & synthesized audio, energy matching, weekly reflection, charts, local NLP, trash recovery, and JSON backups.
Global Overlays Global Search (Ctrl+K / ⌘K), Quick Capture, Setup Wizard, Widget Customizer Instant navigation across every record, rapid inbox capture, persona setup, and dashboard layout tuning.

Getting Started and Dashboard

2.1 First-Time Setup Wizard (3 Steps)

Chapter 3

Managing Tasks and Subtasks

The Tasks workspace supports full task lifecycles: priorities, multi-state workflow status, nested subtasks, tags, filtering, sorting, and linked calendar time blocks.

3.1 Creating or Editing a Task

  1. Open Tasks from the sidebar and click + New Task (or click the pencil icon on an existing task card to edit).
  2. Enter a required Task Title and optional Description.
  3. Choose a Category (e.g., Work & Career, Personal, Education & Study, Operations & Farm, Household & Family, Health & Wellness, Finance & Invoicing, or a custom label).
  4. Set Priority (Low, Medium, High, or Urgent) and Status (Not Started, In Progress, or Completed).
  5. Assign optional Start Date, Due Date, and Due Time, add keyword Tags, and add any checklist Subtasks. Click Save Task.

3.2 Subtasks, Filters, and Sorting

View Filter Tabs
  • All — Every task in your system.
  • Today — Tasks due today.
  • Upcoming — Tasks scheduled for future dates.
  • Overdue — Uncompleted tasks whose due date has passed.
  • Completed — Finished tasks.
Search, Dropdown Filters & Sort
  • Search by title, description, or tag keyword.
  • Filter by Category, Priority, or Status.
  • Sort by Due Date (Asc/Desc), Priority (High→Low / Low→High), or Created Date (Newest/Oldest).
Automatic Calendar Block Synchronization When a task is scheduled as a calendar focus block (via Calendar Smart Scheduling), updating its title, due date, or due time automatically updates the linked calendar block—and marking the task complete removes the scheduled block cleanly.

Calendar, Events and Reminders

Manage single events, recurring series, task deadlines, conflict detection, and in-app reminder alerts across four calendar perspectives.

4.1 Calendar Views & Filters

4 Time Views

Switch seamlessly between Daily, Weekly, Monthly, and Yearly views, and use Prev / Today / Next to navigate dates.

Search & Type Filters

Filter by All, Events Only, or Tasks Only, plus category chips (Work, Personal, Study, Business, Family, Health, Other) and keyword search.

4.2 Creating & Editing Recurring Events

  1. Click + New Event (or click an open time slot / day cell) to open the Event Modal.
  2. Fill in Title, Date, Start/End Time, Category, optional Location, Notes, and Role Tag.
  3. Select a Reminder offset (At time of event, 5m, 15m, 30m, 1h, or 1 day before) to trigger in-app reminder alerts inside Calendar.
  4. Choose a Recurrence rule (Does not repeat, Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly) with an optional end date.
  5. When editing or deleting a recurring event, LifePilot asks you to choose scope: This event only (creates a single-date exception/exclusion), This and following events (splits the series cleanly), or All events in series.

4.3 Smart Scheduling Panel (Inside Calendar)

Click Smart Scheduling in the Calendar toolbar to open the assistant drawer for the selected day:

  • Highlights any same-day overlapping calendar event conflicts.
  • Lists available daytime Free Time Slots (08:00–18:00, minimum 30 minutes).
  • Suggests tailored focus blocks for uncompleted high-priority/today/overdue tasks; clicking Schedule creates or updates a linked Task: <Title> block on your calendar.

Habits and Daily Tracking

Build consistent routines with daily check-ins, live streak counters, and a visual 7-day consistency grid.

5.1 Daily Habit Workflow

  1. Open Habits from the sidebar to view today's completion summary, Longest Active Streak, and the 7-Day Consistency Grid (Mon–Sun).
  2. Click + New Habit to create a habit with a Title, Role/Category Tag (e.g., Health, Work, Study, Routine), and optional Description.
  3. Click Check In on a habit card to mark today complete. Your streak increments by 1 and today's box in the 7-day grid turns emerald green (clicking again toggles it back cleanly).
  4. Use the Edit (pencil) button to update title, tag, or description without resetting your streak or 7-day history, or Delete (trash icon) to move the habit to the Recycle Bin after confirmation.
Dashboard Sync Habits you check off in the Dashboard's Habit Matrix widget immediately synchronize with the Habits view and contribute to your daily Analytics & Focus Score.

Chapter 6

Goals and Progress

Track medium- and long-term milestones across personal, academic, agricultural, or business domains with interactive progress sliders and safe editing.

6.1 Creating, Updating & Deleting Goals

  1. Open Goals from the sidebar to see Active Targets, Average Completion percentage, and your goal cards.
  2. Click + New Goal, fill in Goal Title, Category, Target Date (e.g., Dec 31, End of Q4), and Role Tag, then click Save Milestone.
  3. Drag the Progress Slider (0% to 100%) on any goal card to update completion in real time; changes persist immediately to localStorage.
  4. Click the visible Edit (pencil) button on a goal card (or click the card itself) to open the Edit Strategic Goal modal. Updating title, category, target date, or role tag preserves the goal's original ID and current progress.
  5. Click the Delete (trash) icon to open the confirmation modal. Choosing Cancel preserves the goal unchanged; confirming moves it to the Recycle Bin.
Cross-Module Visibility Your top active goals also appear in the Dashboard's Strategic Goals Overview widget and in the Weekly Life Review snapshot (highlighting goals at 80%+ completion).

Chapter 7

Focus Center and Focus Sounds

Run distraction-free deep work timers with persistent objectives, custom durations, sprint logging, and offline Web Audio ambient soundscapes.

7.1 Pomodoro Timer & Current Focus Objective

  1. Open Focus Center and select a mode: Focus Block (default 25 min), Short Break (default 5 min), or Long Break (default 15 min).
  2. Type what you are working on in the Current Focus Objective input and click Save (or Update, or press Enter). The active objective displays below the timer and persists across page refreshes. Click Clear or the × icon to clear it cleanly.
  3. Click Start Focus to begin the SVG ring countdown. Completing a Focus Block automatically increments your Sprints Logged Today counter (stored by date in lifepilot_focus_session_log).
  4. Under Custom Timer Durations, set any whole number between 1 and 180 minutes for each mode and click Apply Durations (or Restore Defaults for 25m / 5m / 15m).

7.2 Offline Focus Sounds (Web Audio API)

Chapter 8

Energy Planner

Match what you tackle right now to your actual mental and physical energy without modifying your task deadlines.

8.1 How Energy-Aware Matching Works

The Energy Planner uses a deterministic local scoring heuristic (src/utils/energyPlanner.ts) across all uncompleted tasks:

Energy Level Effort Tier Prioritized Scoring Behavior
Low Energy (Drained / Light focus) Light Effort (~15–30 min) Favors quick, single-step tasks with low priority friction and short titles/subtask counts; penalizes deep-focus tasks.
Medium Energy (Steady / Balanced) Moderate Effort (~30–45 min) Balances in-progress tasks, today/overdue obligations, and sustainable steady progress.
High Energy (Peak / Deep work) Deep Focus (~45–60 min) Surfaces urgent, high-priority, and multi-subtask deliverables that benefit most from peak concentration.
Figure 8.1 — Energy-Aware Smart Planner Recommendation Flow
  1. Open Energy Planner and select Low Energy, Medium Energy, or High Energy (persisted in lifepilot_energy_level).
  2. Review the top 6 matches, each showing effort tier, estimated minutes, due date, and exact reasons why it was matched.
  3. Click Refresh Recommendations to cycle tied items, or take direct action: Mark Done, Plan B, or Open Task.

AI Assistant and Global Search

Use the offline Local Assistant to parse plain-English instructions into tasks/events/reminders, compose daily agendas, and resolve role conflicts—or press Ctrl+K / ⌘K to search everywhere at once.

12.1 How the LifePilot Local Assistant Works

Important Technical Disclosure The LifePilot Local Assistant does not connect to external cloud LLM APIs or run a generative neural language model. It runs a local deterministic NLP & scheduling heuristics engine (localNlpParser.ts, agendaComposer.ts, conflictResolver.ts) 100% inside your browser.
  1. Open AI Assistant from the sidebar.
  2. Type a plain-English command such as "Irrigate west field tomorrow at 6 AM.", "Study mathematics for one hour tomorrow.", or "Remind me to submit my assignment on Friday." (or click a suggested prompt chip).
  3. The assistant displays a Review Before Saving preview card where you can edit the detected intent (Task / Calendar Event / Reminder), Title, Date, Time, Role Tag, and Priority, and checks for duplicate records.
  4. Click Confirm & Save (or Discard) so nothing is ever saved without your approval.
  5. Use Compose Daily Agenda to build Morning/Afternoon/Evening time blocks from your real tasks/habits/events (and optionally save blocks to Calendar), or Resolve Role Conflicts to detect and fix clashing events/deadlines across roles.

12.2 Global Search (Ctrl+K / ⌘K)

  • Click the Search... bar in the top Navbar or press Ctrl+K (Windows/Linux) or ⌘K (macOS) from anywhere in LifePilot.
  • Searches live state across Tasks, Calendar Events, Reminders, Habits, Goals, Notes, Quick Captures, and Pages & Views.
  • Use ↑ / ↓ and Enter (or click a result) to jump straight to that view and highlight or open the exact item.

Quick Capture and Notes

Capture fleeting thoughts in seconds without cluttering your task board, and maintain structured long-form reference notes in your Notes Library.

13.1 Quick Captures vs. Actual Tasks (Important Distinction)

Figure 13.1 — Separation Between Quick Captures Inbox and the Tasks Module

Chapter 14

Recycle Bin

Every deletion across LifePilot uses a unified soft-delete Recycle Bin with confirmation dialogs, allowing safe restoration or deliberate permanent removal.

14.1 Supported Entity Recovery

The Recycle Bin stores deleted Tasks (along with any linked calendar blocks), Calendar Events (including individual recurring occurrence exclusions or split series), Reminders, Habits, Goals, Notes, and Quick Captures.

  1. When you delete an item anywhere in LifePilot, a confirmation modal warns you that it will move to Recently Deleted, and a toast notification offers a direct Recycle Bin shortcut.
  2. Open Recycle Bin from the sidebar to filter deleted items by type or search by title, category, role, or deletion source.
  3. Click Restore on a single record to return it to its original module with its original ID, metadata, and relationships intact—or click Restore All / Restore Visible to batch-restore with conflict checks.
  4. Click Delete Forever on an item (or Empty Trash) and confirm in the dialog to permanently purge records from local storage.
No Silent Auto-Purge Items in the Recycle Bin never auto-expire. They remain safely recoverable until you explicitly restore or permanently delete them.

Chapter 16

Data Backup and Restore

Because LifePilot stores everything locally in your browser without a remote database, exporting regular JSON backups protects your work across browser resets or device switches.

16.1 Exporting a Complete Backup

  1. Go to Settings → Local Storage Data Management.
  2. Click Export Backup JSON. LifePilot copies the backup JSON to your clipboard and downloads a timestamped file named lifepilot_backup_YYYY-MM-DD.json.
  3. The export includes your Profile, Tasks, Habits, Goals, Events, Quick Captures, Notes, Recycle Bin records, Widgets, Focus Objective, Focus Durations, Focus Session Log, Energy Level, and Focus Sound Preferences.

16.2 Restoring from a Backup

  1. Click Restore Backup in Settings to open the JSON import box.
  2. Paste the full contents of a previously exported LifePilot JSON backup file.
  3. Click Apply Restore. LifePilot validates the JSON structure before applying—if the format is invalid, your existing data is left untouched and an error message is shown.

16.3 Browser Storage Keys Reference

For technical documentation, LifePilot persists state across 16 keys: lifepilot_profile, lifepilot_theme, lifepilot_tasks, lifepilot_habits, lifepilot_goals, lifepilot_events, lifepilot_quick_captures, lifepilot_widgets, lifepilot_notes, lifepilot_trash, lifepilot_focus_objective, lifepilot_focus_durations, lifepilot_focus_session_log, lifepilot_energy_level, lifepilot_focus_sound_prefs, and lifepilot_ai_chat_history.

Chapter 17

PWA Installation and Offline Usage

LifePilot is configured as a Progressive Web App (PWA) for production deployments, paired with an in-app offline status banner and fallback page.

17.1 Production Build vs. Preview Environment

Environment What Works Actual Platform Limitations
Production Deployment (npm run build served over top-level HTTPS or vite preview) Generates manifest.webmanifest, icons, offline.html, and Workbox sw.js (autoUpdate); supports standalone install and cached offline shell reloads. Requires initial online visit so the browser caches the production bundle and registers the service worker.
Google AI Studio Preview (Vite dev server inside embedded iframe) Once loaded, disconnecting from the network shows the top Offline Mode banner and continues saving all changes to localStorage. Preview Limitation: Cross-origin iframes block standalone PWA install prompts, and service-worker caching is disabled in dev mode (devOptions.enabled: false) so stale dev code is never served. Full offline refresh is not supported in this preview iframe.

17.2 Installing LifePilot in Production

  1. Open your deployed production LifePilot URL directly in a top-level browser tab (Chrome, Edge, or Safari on iOS/macOS).
  2. Desktop / Android Chromium: Open Settings → Offline Readiness & App Installation and click Install LifePilot App (or use your browser's address-bar install icon).
  3. iOS Safari: Tap the browser Share button and choose Add to Home Screen.

Chapter 18

Tips, Troubleshooting and Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common workflow questions and diagnostic steps if something behaves unexpectedly.

18.1 Productivity Tips

  • Press Ctrl+K or ⌘K anytime to jump to any task, note, event, habit, goal, or page without touching your mouse.
  • Keep raw ideas in Quick Capture and promote only concrete actions to Tasks so your daily focus list stays realistic.
  • Pair Energy Planner with Smart Plan B on busy days: pick a task matching your energy, then move it out of calendar collisions cleanly.
  • Export a .json backup weekly before clearing browser history or switching devices.

18.2 Troubleshooting & FAQ

Question / Issue Explanation & Resolution
Why didn't my Focus Objective update while typing? Type your objective in Focus Center and click Save / Update (or press Enter). An amber reminder appears whenever draft edits haven't been saved yet.
Why don't I hear Focus Sounds immediately on page load? Browsers and LifePilot intentionally disable autoplay. Click Play Sound in Focus Center to start the Web Audio engine, and check that Mute is off and Volume > 0%.
I deleted an item by mistake—can I get it back? Yes. Open Recycle Bin from the sidebar and click Restore to bring back tasks, events, habits, goals, notes, reminders, or captures.
Does the AI Assistant send my data to external AI servers? No. It runs a local deterministic NLP parser and heuristics inside your browser with zero external API calls.
Why can't I install the PWA or reload offline inside AI Studio preview? The AI Studio preview runs a development server inside an embedded iframe where browsers restrict PWA installation and dev service-worker caching is disabled. Deploy or preview the production build (npm run build) in a top-level HTTPS tab for full PWA installation and offline shell caching.
